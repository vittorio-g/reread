Parallel analysis systematically underestimates nF:
- nF=4: detects ~2.7 (68%)
- nF=10: detects ~7.2 (72%)
- nF=20: detects ~13.8 (69%)
This means EFA creates FEWER, LARGER factors than the true structure,
which INCREASES within-factor pairs but may mix items from different
true factors. The loading-based weighting partially compensates.

6. STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION
================================================================

WHY EFA WINS AT 60-100 ITEMS:
-------------------------------
With 60-100 items, the factor model is stable enough for EFA to correctly
identify within-factor pairs. The loading-based weights (lambda_i * lambda_j)
provide a SHRINKAGE ESTIMATOR of the true pair correlation:
  - Observed r = true_r + sampling_error
  - Loading product = model-implied r (regularized, less noisy)
This is analogous to James-Stein shrinkage: the model-based estimate is
biased toward the factor structure but has lower MSE than the raw correlation.

WHY WEIGHTED WINS AT <60 ITEMS:
-------------------------------
With <60 items, parallel analysis underestimates nF (detects 2-5 instead of
4-10 true factors). This causes:
  1. Items from different true factors get merged into one EFA factor
  2. Within-factor pairs now include cross-factor pairs (noise)
  3. Loading weights are based on wrong factor assignments
The weighted approach avoids this by using ALL pairs with empirical |r| weights,
which doesn't depend on correct factor extraction.

WHY STANDARD WINS AT >100 ITEMS:
-------------------------------
With 100+ items, the top-3% pairs by |r| are already almost entirely within-factor
(the correlation structure converges). EFA adds estimation noise without new
information, and the loading-based weighting can slightly distort pair importance
when the factor model doesn't perfectly match the true structure.
The standard method's simplicity becomes an advantage: fewer assumptions, fewer
things that can go wrong.

PRACTICAL RECOMMENDATION:
-------------------------------
Optimal auto-switch cascade:
  <= 60 items  -> Weighted (all pairs, |r|-weighted)
  60-100 items -> EFA (within-factor, loading-weighted)
  > 100 items  -> Standard (top-k% by |r|)

However, the EFA advantage at 60-100 items is modest (+0.027 MCC over Standard,
+0.028 over Weighted). A simpler 2-level switch (weighted/coupled at 60 items)
captures most of the benefit. The 3-level switch adds complexity for marginal gain.
